04 · Admin kit

Fields, rows and feedback.

The whole admin is built from these parts. They reuse the site's tokens; the admin adds one colour, Danger #B3261E (6.6:1 on white), only for delete. Inputs get a darker border (#8E92A3, 3.2:1) so the boxes stay visible in sunlight.

Feedback

Saving, undo, delete

The save bar is sticky at the bottom of every form and only turns kesar when something has changed. After saving, a toast confirms and offers undo for 10 seconds.
