Design language · v1.0 · September 2026

Built for the farmer’s phone.

The system behind davadaagro.in: tokens, type and components drawn from the approved prototype. Gujarati-first, calm, and designed at 360 px before anything else.

01

Gujarati first

Gujarati is the default locale. Hindi and English sit one tap away in the header picker — never an equal three-column layout. Copy is written, not translated.

02

Green means WhatsApp

#1DA851 is reserved for inquiry buttons. Nothing else on the site is green, so a visitor learns the rule once and can act without reading.

03

One action per area

Every section has a single primary button. Everything else is a ghost button or an underlined link. White space does the separating.

04

The sanedo is the hero

Flat surfaces, thin lines, no shadows on cards. Product imagery sits on the cool wash so it is always the most colourful thing on screen.

07 · Motion & access

Quiet and reachable

Movement only explains where something came from. Everything works with a thumb, a keyboard and a screen reader.

Focus ring

3 px Kesar 500, 3 px offset. Visible on keyboard focus only.

44
52

Tap targets

44 px minimum for chips, icons and links; 52 px for primary buttons; 48 px in the dock.

250 ms · ease-out · 24 px rise

Sheet & lightbox

Sheets rise from below, lightbox cross-fades. With reduced motion both simply appear.

env(safe-area-inset-*)

Safe areas

Header, dock and lightbox controls respect notches and home indicators. Alt text follows the active language.
